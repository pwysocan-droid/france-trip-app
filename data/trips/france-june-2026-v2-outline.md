# France 2026 — Nice to Cap Ferret to Paris

**June 14 to July 1, 2026.** Three of us, 17 nights, three chapters. Arrive Nice by air on the evening of June 14, depart Paris CDG on July 1 by DL290 at 4pm.

## Why this shape

The trip has three movements. The south (June 14–21, 7 nights) is the modernism arc and the Luberon slow center. Cap Ferret (June 21–27, 6 nights) is the deceleration — friends' house, beach, oysters, no agenda. Paris (June 27 to July 1, 4 nights) is the metropolitan close — Les Puces, museums, neighborhoods, last meals.

The trip is bookended by long-haul flights and stitched together by two TGVs: Narbonne → Bordeaux on June 21, Bordeaux → Paris on June 27.

This document covers the southern leg in detail. Cap Ferret and Paris are sketched at the end and live in chat for now; both will get full treatments in their own time.

The argument of the trip — borrowed from the original modernism route document — is a journey from Cistercian austerity (Le Thoronet, 12th century) through the great modernist statements of the South (Eileen Gray's E-1027, Le Corbusier's Cabanon and Cité Radieuse, Sert's Maeght) and into the village that predates them and outlasts them: Lourmarin. The trip's still center this time isn't a hotel but a stay — three nights at Loïntes Bastides, the private estate just below the hills.

For Nève, there's a cinephile thread: Hitchcock at Cours Saleya, Berri's *Jean de Florette* and *Manon des Sources* mapped onto Vaugines and Mirabeau (a five-minute drive from Lourmarin), and later Varda in Sète if we add her.

## Day 0 — Saturday June 13: Travel

Depart LAX for Paris CDG. Standard transatlantic. Sleep on the plane.

## Day 1 — Sunday June 14: Arrival

**Land Nice 5pm.** Pick up rental car at NCE airport. Drive `Nice → Saint-Paul-de-Vence: ~30 min` via the A8.

**Evening:** easy first dinner at the hotel or in the village. Don't push. The trip starts tomorrow.

**Sleep:** Saint-Paul-de-Vence area ◇ — La Colombe d'Or (legendary, must book months ahead), Le Saint-Paul (Relais & Châteaux), or Hôtel Cantemerle in Vence (quieter, cheaper, 10 minutes away).

## Day 2 — Monday June 15: Cap Moderne, Maeght, Colombe d'Or

The biggest day of the south. Two architectural pilgrimages and a long lunch.

**Morning — Cap Moderne tour.** Drive `Saint-Paul → Roquebrune-Cap-Martin: ~50 min` via the A8. Leave by 8:45am for the 10am French-language tour. Two hours: E-1027 (Eileen Gray, 1929), Le Corbusier's Cabanon (1952), the Étoile de Mer beach restaurant. The high point of the modernism arc. Reserve immediately by emailing capmoderne@monuments-nationaux.fr.

**Lunch — Étoile de Mer.** The beach restaurant directly below E-1027, the same spot Le Corbusier ate with the Rebutato family. Long lunch on the rocks looking back across the bay.

**Afternoon — drive back: `~50 min`** to Saint-Paul. **Fondation Maeght.** Sert's 1964 building, the Miró labyrinth, Calder, Giacometti, the Braque chapel. Two hours minimum.

**Evening — La Colombe d'Or.** Long dinner on the fig-shaded terrace. The art-as-domesticity counterpart to Maeght's monumentality. Reserve 3-4 weeks ahead.

**Sleep:** Saint-Paul-de-Vence (same as Night 1).

## Day 3 — Tuesday June 16: Saint-Paul → Le Thoronet → Cassis

The transition west. One real architectural stop in the middle.

**Morning:** slow breakfast at the hotel. No rushing.

Drive `Saint-Paul → Le Thoronet abbey: ~1.5 hr` via the A8 inland. Aim for 11am arrival when the light through the apse is at its best.

**Late morning — Abbaye du Thoronet.** Twelfth-century Cistercian, ~1.5 hours. The architectural ancestor that Le Corbusier studied before designing La Tourette. The luminous austerity that the trip's modernism inherits.

**Lunch:** light, in the abbey village, or pack from the morning.

Drive `Le Thoronet → Cassis: ~1.5 hr` via the A8.

**Late afternoon:** settle in Cassis. The Calanque de Port-Miou is a 20-minute walk from the village center — easy first calanque if energy remains.

**Evening:** quiet dinner in Cassis. Eat the catch, simply.

**Sleep:** Cassis ◇ — Les Roches Blanches (the address, perched above the harbor with the Cap Canaille view), Hôtel de la Plage Mahogany (beachside, mid-range), or Hôtel Le Liautaud (on the harbor, traditional).

## Day 4 — Wednesday June 17: Marseille day from Cassis

The Marseille day. Cité Radieuse, Tuba, and Maison Empereur — the architecture, the meal, and the singular shop.

**Morning:** drive `Cassis → Marseille: ~30 min`. Park near Cours Saint-Louis (Centre-Bourse).

**Late morning — Cité Radieuse.** Boulevard Michelet, 8th arrondissement, on the way to Les Goudes. Roof terrace is free and open. The post-war "vertical garden city" that completes the Le Corbusier arc you started yesterday at Cap Moderne. ~30-45 minutes plus optional MaMo art center on the roof.

Drive `Cité Radieuse → Tuba Club at Les Goudes: ~25 min` along the corniche.

**Lunch — Tuba Club.** Reserve 3 weeks ahead. Long lunch with swim between courses, 3-4 hours. Bring swimsuits. The Marseille day's anchor.

**Afternoon — Maison Empereur.** Drive back into central Marseille (`~25 min`) to **Maison Empereur** at rue des Récolettes. The eight-generation hardware shop selling everything for living a French domestic life — knives, brushes, soap, copper pots, linens, baskets. Structurally singular, not just an old store. ~1-1.5 hours.

Drive `Marseille → Cassis: ~30 min` along the corniche. Stop at Vallon des Auffes for sunset light if there's time.

**Evening:** quiet dinner in Cassis, or skip if Tuba was big enough.

**Sleep:** Cassis (same as Night 3).

## Day 5 — Thursday June 18: Cassis → Lourmarin

Short transit day. Move from coast to inland.

**Morning:** slow Cassis morning. Coffee on the harbor. Pack at your own pace.

Drive `Cassis → Lourmarin: ~1.5 hr` via the A7 north past Aix.

**Mid-afternoon:** arrive **Loïntes Bastides** ◆. 16 Chemin des Lointes Bastides. Seventeenth-century private estate, five acres, fifteen-meter pool, Aiguebrun river running through, vineyards and ancient olive groves, fifteen-minute walk to the village. Settle in. Swim if it's hot. Three nights.

**Evening:** dinner in Lourmarin village. Something casual — save the Michelin lunch for tomorrow.

**Sleep:** Loïntes Bastides ◆ — Night 1 of 3.

## Day 6 — Friday June 19: Lourmarin slow + cinephile half-day

The trip's slow center. The day everything decompresses.

**Morning:** coffee in the village. Walk Lourmarin without crowds. **Camus's grave** in the cemetery above town — quiet, unmarked, a simple stone. If Nève has read a few pages of *L'Étranger* before the trip, the visit becomes literary.

**Mid-morning to early afternoon — the cinephile half-day.** The *Jean de Florette / Manon des Sources* pilgrimage:

- **Vaugines** (`~5 min` from Lourmarin): église Saint-Barthélémy (the wedding, the final sermon), the cemetery (the bench scene). 30-45 minutes.
- **Mirabeau** (`~30 min` from Vaugines): the fountain (the dry one Manon caused), Café de la Fontaine, the school-now-mairie. Coffee at the café. 45 minutes.

Drive `→ Cucuron: ~30 min`.

**Lunch — La Petite Maison de Cucuron.** Place de l'Étang. Eric Sapet, one Michelin star. Reserve 2-3 weeks ahead. 2.5-3 hours.

**Late afternoon — Place de l'Étang.** Long café on the platanes-shaded square next door. The mirror of water, the slow light. The purest Provence-afternoon of the trip.

Drive `Cucuron → Loïntes Bastides: ~15 min`.

**Evening:** dinner in or at the estate. Don't pile big meals.

**Sleep:** Loïntes Bastides ◆ — Night 2 of 3.

## Day 7 — Saturday June 20: pure Loïntes day

The slowest day of the trip, deliberately.

Slow morning at the estate. Pool, river, garden. Lourmarin's Saturday market in the village (the smaller one — Friday's is the big one, but you arrive after it). Lunch at the estate or a casual village bistrot. Long pool / river afternoon. Possibly drive 30 minutes to one of the perched villages — Bonnieux, Ménerbes, Gordes — for golden hour. Or don't.

This is the day that earns the third night at Loïntes Bastides. The trip has slack here on purpose.

**Evening:** last dinner in the Luberon. Lourmarin village has good options.

**Sleep:** Loïntes Bastides ◆ — Night 3 of 3.

## Day 8 — Sunday June 21: Train to Cap Ferret

The transition west.

**Morning:** slow breakfast at Loïntes Bastides. Pack. Leave by ~9:30am to give buffer.

Drive `Lourmarin → Narbonne: ~3 hr` via the A7 + A9.

Drop the rental car at Narbonne station. Verify drop-off contractually with Avis or Hertz before booking — Narbonne isn't always default, but most chains permit it.

**TGV Narbonne → Bordeaux:** `~3 hr` direct. Book mid-March 2026 when SNCF's 90-day window opens.

**Pick up rental car at Bordeaux Saint-Jean.** Drive `Bordeaux → Cap Ferret: ~1 hr` via A630 + D106. Arrive evening at the friends' house on Plage Américaine.

End of the southern leg. Cap Ferret begins.

## Cap Ferret and Paris

These chapters are sketched in chat and will get their own outlines later. Six nights at friends' house at Plage Américaine — beach, oysters at Chez Hortense, daily Lège-Cap-Ferret market, Dune du Pilat by water taxi, all dinners cooked at home. Then four nights in Paris in a 7th-arrondissement AirBnB — Les Puces with Nève, Marais museums (Picasso and Alaïa), Bourse de Commerce, Le Bon Marché, restaurants in the unfussy register (Le Duc, Maison Sota, Chez Georges, Lao Siam, with Telescope and Dreamin' Man for coffee).

## What this draft deliberately doesn't include

**La Celle.** The Lucrèce de Barras suite at Hostellerie de l'Abbaye de la Celle was the still-center of v1 and is genuinely missed. It's been deferred to a future trip — La Celle is the kind of place you go *to*, on its own weekend, rather than fitting into a moving itinerary. The slow center of v2 is Loïntes Bastides instead.

**Sète and the Languedoc coast.** v1 ended at Sète with oysters and Valéry's grave; v2 ends earlier and routes north to Cap Ferret instead. The Languedoc chapter is gone for this trip.

**Aix-en-Provence and Vasarely.** Considered as a Day 7 half-day; chosen instead to keep Day 7 as pure Loïntes. The Vasarely Foundation and Cézanne's atelier (which reopens July 2026, after this trip) wait for next time.

**Villa Noailles in Hyères.** The missing first chapter of the modernism arc, not on the route.

**Frank Gehry's Luma in Arles.** Worth seeing but doesn't fit this trip's specific argument.
